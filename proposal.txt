Project Overview: 
My project creates a bee hive simulation, designed to predict future changes within bee hives over 5 years. This software will illustrate the life cycles of bee hives,
either through natural or man-made causes.

Implementation:
I plan on using a std:map to store and manage data between different hives and bees in different situations. This map will categorize the bees into three std::lists:
worker drones, queen bees, and dead bees. The map will have the bee hive locations as its key. The map's value will be an array of these three lists.

One map will be for a wild bee hive, another for a beehive close to wild humans, and the last will be for a beekeeper hive. 

Simulated events:
The simulation will focus on three events: new queen bee births, colony food sources, and random enemy encounters. At any given time period, a new queen bee can split the hive
or die out, external factors can cause migration, or a sickness can affect a beehive.
